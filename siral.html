The Guiding Light

In classrooms bright, a gentle grace, You light the path in every place. A compass true, a patient hand, The finest teacher in the land. Your lessons learned, not just of books, But kindness in your smiling looks. You see the strength, the dream untold, Turning hesitant hearts to bold. With wisdom deep and patience kind, You shape and mold each eager mind. You banish doubt and quell all fear, And teach us how to persevere. For all the seeds of thought you sow, The knowledge that you help us grow, A heartfelt thanks, a happy cheer, We wish you joy throughout the year!
