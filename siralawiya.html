Happeee Teacha's Dae Sire!

From your ever-grateful students, BSCS III-B Sir, as we celebrate this special day for teachers, we wish to take a moment to express our profound gratitude for your mentorship, guidance, and constant inspiration throughout our collegiate journey. For three long years, from the moment we commenced our college studies until the present, you have been one of the few instructors who has remained by our side, diligently shaping our intellectual abilities, challenging our limits, and fostering our growth not only as students but also as individuals. We recall vividly the experience of our first year. At that time, everything was novel, perplexing, and at times overwhelming. However, your composed and patient instructional approach made those difficult lessons more readily comprehensible. You never diminished our efforts when we faced difficulties. Instead, you instilled in us the confidence to persevere until successful. You consistently reminded us that learning is a process, that errors are integral to growth, and that every challenge can be overcome through concerted effort and self-belief. Now, as we are in our third year, we recognize the significant impact you have had on us. You have transcended the role of a mere teacher of System Fundamentals and Computational Science. You have functioned as a mentor who genuinely invested in our progress. You imparted lessons that extend well beyond coding, logic, and computation. You instructed us in patience, respect, teamwork, and responsibility. You demonstrated the meaning of professional passion and the fulfillment derived from sharing knowledge with others. Your dedication, Sir, is never overlooked. We observe the effort you commit to every discussion, the time devoted to lesson preparation, and the patience you extend to each of us. Even when the subject matter becomes highly complex or when the class is somewhat disruptive, you consistently manage to guide us with kindness and understanding. You transform the learning process into an enjoyable endeavor rather than a source of apprehension. For this, we shall remain perpetually grateful. We thank you for believing in us, particularly during moments when we struggled to maintain faith in ourselves. Thank you for your continued patience when our comprehension was slow, for offering second opportunities upon failure, and for consistently motivating us to achieve greater excellence. Thank you for being the type of educator who teaches not merely for grades, but for personal development and sustained learning. You have become a fundamental part of our college experience—a presence in every semester, every project, every moment of levity, and even every period of academic pressure. We have acquired substantial knowledge from you, not solely concerning computers and systems, but also regarding humility, respect, and perseverance. On this special occasion, we wish to convey the depth of our appreciation. You are a primary reason why we maintain our commitment to this course and why we strive for improvement daily. Your lessons will endure with us, long after our departure from this institution. We extend our wishes for good health, unending happiness, and continued success in your life and career. May you continue to inspire many more students like us—students who will always remember the teacher who guided them with patience, wisdom, and heart. Thank you for everything, Sir Abramar. You are truly exceptional. With love, respect, and gratitude, Your BSCS III-B Family
