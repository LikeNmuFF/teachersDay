Igniting Our BSCS Journey

Dear Ma'am Matheen, Happy Teachers' Day! On this special day, we, your 3rd Year Computer Science Set B students, wanted to reach out and express our sincere gratitude and appreciation. Thank you for your continuous guidance, patience, and support. This thanks isn't just for this year, but ever since we started our 2nd-year journey with you, when you were our instructor for Logic Gates. Looking back, one of the most unforgettable experiences we had was creating those unique robotics projects for our exhibition. That achievement was only possible because of you. Your encouragement, your trust in our abilities, and your dedication helped us learn so much more than what was written in our modules. You inspired us to think creatively, to challenge ourselves, and to see technology not just as a subject, but as a passion worth pursuing. More than being an excellent instructor, you’ve always been such a supportive adviser, especially during our most challenging times, like when we had difficulties collecting funds or organizing our class activities. You always guided us with kindness and understanding. You reminded us that teamwork, communication, and responsibility are just as important as academic success. Now that we are in our 3rd year, we are truly grateful and proud to still have you as our adviser. Your dedication, compassion, and consistent support continue to inspire us to do our best. We hope to not only succeed for ourselves but also to make you proud. Thank you, Ma'am Matheen, for being more than just a teacher to us. You are our mentor, our motivator, and a big, important part of our journey as Computer Science students. We are blessed to be guided by someone who truly cares and believes in what we can become.
